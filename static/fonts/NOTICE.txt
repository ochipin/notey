All 48 icon glyphs were built from official Google SVGs at the revision
above. The former mixed-source font was completely replaced; none of its
outlines are included. Proxmox, Tux/Linux, Docker, and Windows remain excluded.

Modified by the Notey contributors on 2026-09-16: selected artwork was
converted to font outlines with FontTools 4.65.0, scaled to 1024 units per em,
and transformed from SVG coordinates to the font's upward Y axis. Ascent is
960 and descent is -64. Metrics were normalized, curves were converted to
TrueType-compatible outlines, and the glyphs were assigned Notey's existing
private-use codepoints and packed into WOFF. Original SVGs are preserved
unchanged in the theme source.

The icomoon.woff filename and icomoon CSS family name are retained for
compatibility. The replacement font is generated with FontTools, not IcoMoon.
